Usage rules

DO

  • Lock the disclosure footer on every image post.
  • One headline idea per card. Two lines max.
  • Use the serif italic accent on the most important word only.
  • Keep numbers in Geist Mono.

DON'T

  • Use buy / sell / target / SL language anywhere.
  • Name specific stocks as recommendations — only as breadth/leadership context.
  • Add red gradients or hype colors. Stay on the green/neutral palette.
  • Use emoji except sparingly in tweet copy (never inside the image).

SAFE AREAS

  • Header: keep all text in the right 75% — avatar overlaps bottom-left.
  • Header: ~60px crop on mobile top, ~110px crop bottom.
  • Avatar: keep mark inside the inner 80% — circular crop hides corners.
  • Posts: 80px page margin, 56px from any image edge.
