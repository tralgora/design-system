Voice — what we say, what we don't

Say

  • "Strength is broadening into Defence and Capital Goods."
  • "Breadth above 200d is at 64% — leadership has space to extend."
  • "FII selling absorbed by DII; macro pressure is neutral."
  • "This is a framework, not a tip."
  • "Educational and informational only."

Never say

  • "Buy XYZ at ₹420, target 480, SL 405."
  • "Sure-shot multibagger for 2026."
  • "Guaranteed 12% in 30 days."
  • "Our calls have 92% accuracy."
  • "Personalised investment advice."
