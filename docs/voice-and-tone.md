# Voice & tone

Tralgora is a market intelligence radar — calm, precise, and structural. It does not hype. It does not promise. It does not give tips.

## Posture

- **Operator-to-operator.** Talk to someone who already trades or invests. Don't over-explain.
- **Structural, not narrative.** "Breadth is broadening" beats "stocks went up because the budget was good."
- **Specific numbers.** Real values (`+1.4σ`, `64%`, `₹1,205 cr`) over vague language ("strong," "weak").
- **One italic word per page.** Use Instrument Serif italic to highlight the pivot — _structure_, _moving_, _broadening_, _absorbed_. Never twice in the same headline.

## Banned language (public surfaces)

Do not use any of the following in copy, blog posts, social, emails, or in-app strings on **public mode**:

- Buy now / sell now / hold
- Target · stop-loss · entry/exit on a specific name
- Guaranteed return · sure-shot · multibagger · 10× · 2×
- "Our calls have N% accuracy"
- Personalised investment advice / "based on your portfolio…"

Use these instead:

| Don't say | Say |
|---|---|
| "Buy HAL at ₹4,200" | "Defence is leading — HAL is the highest-RS constituent." |
| "Multibagger picks 2026" | "Themes with structural tailwinds." |
| "92% accuracy track record" | "Daily post-market review of the framework's read." |
| "Sure-shot intraday calls" | "Intraday context — themes, breadth, macro before the open." |

## Disclaimer block (paste verbatim)

> Tralgora is for educational and informational purposes only. Nothing on this website should be treated as investment advice, stock recommendation, or a buy/sell/hold call. Market data and commentary are provided to help users understand market structure. Please consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser before making investment decisions.

This block must appear on every public page near the footer. In emails, it sits at the bottom. In WhatsApp messages, an abbreviated form ("Educational use only — not investment advice.") is acceptable for length, but the full block must link from any landing surface.

## Italic accent — examples

- See where the market is _really_ moving.
- Different styles. Same underlying _structure_.
- Strength is _broadening_.
- FII selling _absorbed_ by DII.
- Process, not _posture_.

One per heading. Never on body copy.
